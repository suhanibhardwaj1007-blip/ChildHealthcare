Symptom Triage & Pediatric First Aid

Quick reference guide for common childhood ailments, warning flags, and emergency first aid responses.

🚨

Immediate Pediatric Emergency Red Flags (Seek Urgent Hospital Care)

If your child experiences any of the following symptoms, go immediately to the nearest Emergency Pediatric Hospital or dial 108:

  • Infant Under 3 Months: Any rectal fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher.
  • Breathing Distress: Rapid breathing, chest ribs sinking in (retractions), wheezing, or blue tint around lips.
  • Severe Lethargy: Unusually floppy, unable to wake up, or not responding to voice/touch.
  • Dehydration: No wet diaper in 8+ hours, crying without tears, sunken soft spot (fontanelle).
  • Seizures or Stiff Neck: Accompanied by extreme light sensitivity or a dark purple rash that doesn't fade when pressed.

🩺 Common Pediatric Symptoms & Home Care

🌡️ High Fever
Moderate Caution

Fever is the immune system's natural reaction against infections. Normal body temp is 98.6°F (37°C).

Home Care: Keep the child hydrated with water, ORS, or milk. Dress in light cotton clothes. Use lukewarm sponge baths (never cold water/alcohol). Administer Paracetamol only as prescribed per child's weight.
💨 Infant Colic & Gas
Mild • Self-Limiting

Unexplained prolonged crying in healthy infants under 4 months, usually peaking in the evening.

Relief Technique: Gently cycle baby's legs like a bicycle. Hold baby tummy-down along your forearm. Burp after every feed and ensure proper latch to avoid swallowing air.
🤧 Cold & Congestion
Mild • Viral

Runny or blocked nose, mild cough, sneezing. Usually caused by common seasonal viruses.

Home Care: Use 1-2 drops of pediatric normal saline nasal drops before feedings and naps. Run a cool mist humidifier in the bedroom. Elevate mattress slightly.
💧 Diarrhea & Stomach Bug
Hydration Vital

Watery stools, nausea. The primary danger with pediatric diarrhea is rapid dehydration.

ORS Protocol: Administer WHO-formula Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) in small, frequent sips after every loose stool. Continue breast milk and avoid fruit juices or sugary drinks.

🚑 Step-by-Step Emergency First Aid

Emergency Protocol

👶 Infant Choking Relief (Under 1 Year)

Step 1: 5 Back Blows

Lay infant facedown along your forearm, supporting jaw. Deliver 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades with heel of hand.

Step 2: 5 Chest Thrusts

Turn infant face up on your thigh. Place 2 fingers in center of breastbone and press down sharply 5 times.

Step 3: Repeat & Call 108

Repeat 5 blows / 5 thrusts until object dislodges or baby cries. Call emergency services immediately.

Thermal First Aid

🔥 Minor Burns & Scalds Treatment

Immediately hold the burned area under cool, gentle running tap water for 15 to 20 minutes. Never apply ice, butter, oils, or toothpaste. Cover loosely with a clean, non-stick sterile gauze or plastic cling film and visit your doctor.
